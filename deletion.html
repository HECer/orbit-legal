Data Deletion

As of 11 October 2026 — ORBIT 1.2.1

ORBIT has no player account. Progress and settings are stored on your device.

Delete local game data

Open ORBIT, choose Settings, then RESET LOCAL GAME DATA and confirm. This removes the local game profile and progress. Your app-store purchase remains attached to your store account and can be restored. Device or cloud backups are managed separately by your operating-system provider.

Withdraw analytics consent or request analytics deletion

In builds with optional Unity Analytics, open Settings and turn OPTIONAL ANALYTICS off to stop further collection. Choose DELETE ANALYTICS DATA to withdraw consent and request deletion through Unity's SDK. An internet connection is needed for the remote request. Local progress is unaffected. If these controls are unavailable, optional analytics are disabled in that build.

Other privacy requests

Email info@totalcreations.de with the subject “ORBIT privacy request”. State which data or service your request concerns and the app version/platform. Do not send passwords, store payment details or identity documents with your initial request. We will ask for only the information needed to locate and verify your request.

ORBIT does not store your app-store payment card information. Apple, Google and other platform providers handle their own transaction, advertising and operating-system data. An in-game reset or Unity deletion request does not erase those providers' records. Their privacy choices and support processes remain available separately.

See the Privacy Policy for contact details, services and applicable rights.

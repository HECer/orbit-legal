5. Advertising

The Android 1.2 implementation supports a one-time, non-consumable Remove All Ads purchase. The store shows the price before checkout. Once purchased, both automatic and optional ads are disabled; optional reward bonuses remain available without watching a video. Restore Purchases retrieves the entitlement from the original store account. This purchase does not transfer between different app stores.

When configured in a release, occasional interstitial ads may appear when you leave the results of a successful run. They never interrupt an active battle or the tutorial and are not shown after a loss. The implementation applies session limits and time gaps. Gameplay does not require a purchase.

If advertising is enabled in a release, an optional rewarded video may be offered after a completed run. It starts only after you choose it. The Game may grant the result reward once for a fully completed video; the amount is shown before playback. There are no ad interruptions during an active battle. The offer can be unavailable because of region, privacy choice, connectivity, or inventory. No particular ad or reward is guaranteed.
